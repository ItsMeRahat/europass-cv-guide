GET STARTED

What is Europass CV and where to create it?

Europass CV is a standardised CV format recognised by the European Union (EU). It is used across Europe for jobs, higher education, scholarships, internships and other academic or professional applications. It can be created online, free of charge, from Bangladesh.

This is the official Europass website of the European Union. No third-party website is needed.

Create Your Europass CV → https://europa.eu/europass/en/create-europass-cv
HOW IT WORKS

Build your Europass profile first

The Europass CV is one of the best-known CV formats in Europe. It is designed to be easy to use and familiar to employers and education institutions.

  • 1Create your Europass profile with information about your education, training, work experience and skills.
  • 2Once your profile is complete, select the information you want to include in a CV.
  • 3Choose your favourite design. Europass uses your selected profile information to prepare the CV.
  • 4You can create multiple CVs from the same profile for different applications.
Europass says CVs can be created, stored and shared in 31 languages. You can download your CV, store it in your Europass Library, or share it with employers, EURES or other job boards.
STEP 1

Access the Website

  • 1Go to the Official Website link above.
  • 2Select English as the language.
  • 3Sign in or create your free Europass account to build and save your profile.
  • 4Open your Europass profile and start adding your information. Follow the on-screen prompts to create your CV.
STEP 2

Fill in Personal Information

You will need to provide the following:

  • Full Name
  • Professional Title
  • Address
  • Phone Number (+880)
  • Email Address
  • Date of Birth (if required)
  • Nationality
  • Professional Photograph (if required)
STEP 3

Education & Training

Enter your educational qualifications. Put your most recent degree first.

InformationExample
DegreeBachelor’s / Master’s
InstitutionUniversity Name
Study Period2020–2024
SubjectSociology
ResultCGPA / GPA
STEP 4

Work Experience

For each job, include:

  • Job Title
  • Employer Name
  • Location
  • Employment Period
  • Main Responsibilities
  • Achievements
If you have no work experience, you can add Internships, Volunteering or Academic Projects.
STEP 5

Personal Skills

Include a few key points here:

  • Language Skills – English, Bangla, etc.
  • Digital Skills – MS Word, Excel, PowerPoint, etc.
  • Communication Skills
  • Teamwork
  • Problem-solving
  • Organisational Skills
STEP 6

About Me / Profile Summary

This is an important part of your CV. Briefly present:

  • Who you are
  • Your skills
  • Your experience
  • Your career goals
STEP 7

Choose a Template & Download

  • 1Choose a CV design.
  • 2Carefully review all information.
  • 3Select PDF format.
  • 4Download and save it.
